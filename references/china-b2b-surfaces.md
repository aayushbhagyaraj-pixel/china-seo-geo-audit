# Where a Chinese B2B buyer actually looks

A `.cn` site plus Baidu organic is a fraction of the discovery path. The surfaces
below are where the buyer verifies a foreign supplier, and where a brand's
absence gets filled by resellers, unauthorised agents and decade-old articles.
Audit presence and accuracy on each; do not mandate a content programme on any of
them without an observed citation or a stated buyer need.

## 1. Baidu-owned surfaces that sit above organic results

| Surface | What it is | Foreign brand reality | Audit check |
|---|---|---|---|
| **百度爱采购 (Aicaigou)** | Baidu's B2B procurement marketplace. Listings appear as enhanced cards in the SERP for supplier and product queries | Requires a Chinese business entity and a paid membership. Foreign brands appear through distributors, or not at all | Who is listed for the category and the brand name? Is any listing an unauthorised seller? |
| **百家号 (Baijiahao)** | Baidu's publishing platform. Articles receive privileged placement in mobile results | Registration needs real-name verification; enterprise accounts need a Chinese licence | Does anyone publish about the brand there? Is it accurate? |
| **百度百科 (Baike)** | Encyclopedia entry, near the top of any brand query, and a grounding source for ERNIE | Editable by anyone; claiming it requires verification | Does the entry exist, is it claimed, is the first line correct? |
| **百度知道 / 文库 / 经验** | Q&A, documents, how-to | Open | Are the answers about the brand accurate? Who wrote them? |
| **精选笔记 / 百度有驾** | Aggregated notes and the automotive vertical | Passive | Whether they appear for the target queries and what they say |

## 2. Procurement platforms

| Platform | Buyer | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **1688** | Domestic wholesale and component procurement | Foreign brands appear via resellers. Check for "原装", "正品" claims and who makes them |
| **慧聪网 (HC360)** | Industrial B2B | Legacy but still indexed heavily |
| **中国制造网 (Made-in-China)** | Export-facing, Chinese suppliers to foreign buyers | Rarely relevant for a foreign brand selling into China |
| **阿里巴巴国际站** | Export-facing | Same |

The recurring finding is an unauthorised agent claim: a trading company describing
itself as 中国区总代理 (China general agent) for a brand that has a different
distributor. It surfaces in Baidu, in AI answers and in procurement platforms.
Confirm the real agent structure with the client and have the false claim
corrected at source.

## 3. Search inside apps

| Surface | Why it matters | Audit check |
|---|---|---|
| **微信搜一搜 (WeChat search)** | Searches 公众号 articles, mini-programs and video. A large share of B2B research happens here, and 公众号 content is otherwise invisible to Baidu | Does the brand's 公众号 exist, is it verified, when did it last post, does it appear for the brand name |
| **抖音搜索 (Douyin)** | Now a primary search surface for younger buyers and for product demonstration | Brand or product videos present? Who else's? |
| **小红书搜索 (Xiaohongshu)** | Design, fashion, interiors, consumer electronics decision-makers research here | Presence, sentiment, unauthorised sellers |
| **知乎 (Zhihu)** | Technical comparison and "is it worth it" questions | Are the comparison questions answered, and by whom |
| **哔哩哔哩 (Bilibili)** | Long-form technical video | Presence |

Sogou indexes 公众号 articles and is integrated with WeChat, which is why Sogou
matters far more than its market share suggests for a brand distributing through
WeChat.

## 4. Trust and contact conventions on the site itself

Western B2B sites lean on a form. Chinese buyers expect to verify the entity and
reach a person quickly. Check for:

- Chinese legal entity name and address, consistent with the 天眼查 / 企查查 registry
  record. A mismatch is a finding the buyer will find first.
- 营业执照 or 资质 (business licence, certifications) shown or linked.
- ICP filing number in the footer if hosted in the mainland; if not, say so plainly.
- A 400 or +86 phone number that is answered in Chinese.
- WeChat: an official account QR, and increasingly 企业微信 (WeCom) for direct
  contact.
- 在线客服 (live chat) where the buyer expects it; a slow or foreign-hosted widget
  is worse than none.
- A named contact or department for the enquiry, not a generic inbox.

Phase 6 tracks form delivery. Add these paths to the same measurement; a buyer who
cannot find a phone number leaves before the form.

## 5. Brand SERP defence

For the brand name, brand + 官网 and brand Chinese name, record: paid ads and who
bought them, 品牌专区 presence, which owned property ranks first, and every domain
above it. Third parties bidding on the brand term and domain squatters using the
name are brand-protection findings; report them in the audit and hand them to the
client's legal or brand team.

## 6. Reputation and safety flags

- 舆情: search the brand name with 骗子, 投诉, 假货, 质量问题. Record what appears
  and its date. Do not draw conclusions from one post.
- Baidu 安全检测: a 风险提示 (risk warning) on the site's result suppresses
  clicks regardless of rank. Check the brand SERP for it.

## 7. Compliance beyond ICP and advertising law

Flag, do not conclude. These belong to Chinese counsel.

| Law | Trigger on a typical B2B site |
|---|---|
| **个人信息保护法 (PIPL)** | Any form collecting name, phone or email from a mainland user and sending it to a server outside China is a cross-border transfer. Separate consent and a stated purpose are required; the standard contract or assessment route depends on volume |
| **数据安全法 / 网络安全法** | Data classification, and network security obligations for operators in the mainland |
| **等级保护 2.0** | Applies to systems hosted in the mainland |
| **电子商务法** | If the site sells or takes orders |
| **广告法** | Superlatives in copy (covered in technical-onpage.md) |

A site can be technically perfect for Baidu and legally unshippable. Note the
trigger and the owner; do not write the legal conclusion into an SEO deliverable.
