# Deliverables, priorities and staging

## Finding format

Every finding carries all seven fields. A finding missing the consequence or the
acceptance check is a note, not a finding.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Finding** | What is true, in one sentence, with its claim label |
| **Evidence** | The observation: tool, exact target, timestamp with timezone, result. Link the raw file |
| **Business consequence** | What it costs the client in buyer behaviour or revenue — not "hurts SEO" |
| **Action** | The specific change, at the level the owner can execute |
| **Owner** | Who does it: client IT, the agency, the CMS vendor, the China team |
| **Priority** | Critical / High / Medium / Low, per the ordering below |
| **Acceptance check** | The observation that will show it is done |

## Priority ordering

1. **Broken contact paths and access failures.** A form that cannot submit and a
   page that does not load outrank everything. These are revenue, not ranking.
2. **Accurate identity.** The right division, scope and business model — in Chinese.
   Everything downstream compounds an identity error.
3. **Buyer-content gaps.** The destination pages a procurement buyer needs and
   cannot currently find.
4. **Measured distribution tests.** Channels justified by buyer need or by an
   observed citation, tested and measured — not a standing channel mandate.

Within a tier, order by evidence strength and by effort.

## Staging

Use stages with **completion criteria**, not invented dates:

| Stage | Completion criterion |
|---|---|
| **Fix** | Access, rendering and contact paths verified working from mainland networks |
| **Identity** | Correct division, scope and Chinese naming present on owned pages and observable in assistant answers |
| **Answers** | Buyer questions answered on readable, server-rendered pages with units, methods and dates |
| **Proof** | Approved specifications, cases and partner evidence published and referenceable |
| **Recurring operation** | Panel re-run on a defined cadence; qualified inquiries tracked to outcome |

Identity work can proceed alongside fixes. Add dates only where the agreed scope
needs them.

Do **not**: invent a fixed delivery schedule, invent a page-count target, promise a
ranking, citation or revenue uplift, or mandate a new platform account by default.

## Remaining-data list

Close every deliverable with what is still missing and who holds it. This is what
makes the audit honest and what scopes the next phase:

- Owner analytics access (and whether Chinese traffic is measurable at all today)
- Baidu Search Resource Platform exports
- Consumer-assistant runs still pending or blocked
- Mainland browser tests
- Approved specifications and claims
- Lead outcomes and CRM data
- Anything marked **blocked** in the findings, with the blocker named

## Bilingual deliverables

Where the deliverable is English-facing but the evidence is Chinese, put an English
translation beside every Chinese prompt, query, term and quoted excerpt. Keep the
raw Chinese snapshots unmodified in the evidence appendix.

## Format

This skill produces the audit: findings, evidence, priorities and the
remaining-data list. The format of the client artifact is decided outside it.
Whatever the format, keep the evidence companion as a separate document so that
findings which do not fit a fixed template are not lost.
